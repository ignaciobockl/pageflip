# Governance

PageFlip follows a benevolent founder model.

## Decision authority

- Final technical, product, release, and branding decisions belong to Ignacio Bockl.
- Issues may be discussed publicly, but implementation starts after explicit approval.
- Pull requests require owner approval before merge.

## Contributions

- External contributions are welcome after an approved issue exists.
- The owner may request changes, reject proposals, or defer work without obligation.
- Governance, trademark, licensing, and release policies override informal discussion.

## Releases

- Only the owner may authorize stable releases, package publication, and governance changes.

## Enforcement

- Repository automation may block changes that do not satisfy these rules.
